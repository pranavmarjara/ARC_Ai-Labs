Speech, not guessed. measured.

Every sentence is a feat of coordination.

Which is why neurological conditions leave their mark on speech.

Yet, speech is still judged by ear.

Ears can lie. Numbers don't.

ARC-1 helps with exactly that.

Breath. Muscle. Timing. Thought.

All arriving at once, in a single word.

ARC-1 is a headset designed to hear only the person speaking, filtering out the noise around them.

From a few short speech tasks, it calculates the speech biomarkers and turns them into clear graphs, so changes are easy to see and analyse over time.

Built like an instrument.

Too often the patient has to fit the tool. ARC-1 is built the other way round: every part of it is designed around the patient's comfort, so the measurement fits them.

Made to fit the patient

A working instrument, a filed patent, and the study that proves it is designed.

  • Working instrument, 14 measures with documented mathematics
  • Patent filed · SISFS funded
  • Incubated at TBIF, IIT Ropar and CCIE
  • 11 lab demonstrations secured
  • Ethics approval and test licence in progress
